# Carousel deck — "A mirror, not a window"

*12 slides, one idea each. Quotes pulled from LINKEDIN-honest.md / -honest-post.md and
tightened for card size (one sentence, ~90 chars max per card). Cohesion devices: the
mirror/window motif opens, haunts, and closes the deck; every claim card is followed by
its proof card; the three rules numbered on slide 2 return as the three closers.*

---

**SLIDE 1 — Cover**
# I built an I Ching app that refuses to tell your fortune.
### Then I made it prove it.
*(footer: 12 cards · honest build-in-public)*

**SLIDE 2 — The hook**
> The book is a mirror, not a window.
> Almost every app built around it sells you a window — and wants your email first.

**SLIDE 3 — The three rules**
# Three rules, set before line one of code:
### 1. Reflection, never prediction.
### 2. Private by construction.
### 3. Honest mechanics.

**SLIDE 4 — Rule 1, claimed**
> The text holds your question back to you in different light.
> The meaning was always yours.

**SLIDE 5 — Rule 1, proved**
> No predictions. No advice. All content attributed quotations —
> Legge, 1882, public domain.

**SLIDE 6 — Rule 2, claimed**
> Your question dies with the tab.
> It has never existed anywhere else.

**SLIDE 7 — Rule 2, proved**
> One file. ~105 KB. Zero runtime dependencies.
> Works from your Downloads folder with the network off.
*(pairlabel: CSP connect-src 'none' — nothing can leave the page)*

**SLIDE 8 — Rule 3, claimed**
> No secure random source? No cast.
> No quiet fallback. Ever.

**SLIDE 9 — Rule 3, proved**
> Trust you can't check is just branding.
> So the rules became a verifier that fails the build.

**SLIDE 10 — The tools, honestly**
> "Secure" stopped being an opinion
> and became a grep with a non-zero exit code.

**SLIDE 11 — The honest costs**
> No CI. No users. No A/B tests.
> The product doc forbids inventing testimonials.

**SLIDE 12 — Closer / CTA**
> A cast you didn't mean, in an app that didn't mean it,
> isn't a mirror. It's a slot machine with a serif font.
### Say what it does. Do what you said. Let anyone check.
